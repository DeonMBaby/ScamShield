Acted fast? Money can sometimes be recovered.

The first hour matters most. Do these in order:

  1. Call your bank immediately and ask them to block the card, UPI or account and flag the transaction.
  2. Call the National Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930 (India) and report the fraud right away.
  3. File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in with screenshots, transaction IDs, phone numbers and links.
  4. Change passwords and PINs for anything you shared, and turn on two-step verification.
  5. Don't delete evidence. Keep chats, call logs and receipts.

Golden rules

  • Real banks never ask for OTP, PIN, CVV or passwords.
  • You never pay to receive money, a prize or a refund.
  • Urgency is a weapon. Pause and verify through the official app or number.
