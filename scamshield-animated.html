Acted fast? Money can sometimes be recovered.

  1. Call your bank now (number on your card or app) to block the card, UPI or account.
  2. Call the Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 (India) and report it right away.
  3. File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in with screenshots and transaction IDs.
  4. Change passwords and PINs you shared; turn on two-step verification.
  5. Keep all evidence and warn family and friends.
